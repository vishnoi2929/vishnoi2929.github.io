Privacy Policy for ColorWorld

Last updated: July 15, 2026

At ColorWorld, accessible from the Google Play Store, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy document contains details on the types of information collected and recorded by ColorWorld and how we use it.

Important Summary: ColorWorld is designed to be an offline-first creative studio. Your drawings, painting strokes, and color preferences are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database and are NOT transmitted to any external servers.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or request any personally identifiable information (such as your name, phone number, physical address, or email address) to use the core features of the ColorWorld app.

2. Google AdMob (Advertising)

Our app uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob uses cookies and device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to customize and serve personalized ads.

You can manage your device's ad settings to opt-out of personalized tracking through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). For details on how Google uses your data, please read the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

3. Log Files

ColorWorld follows a standard procedure of using local log files when errors or crashes occur. These files are used strictly for debugging purposes and are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. Children's Privacy

ColorWorld is suitable for children and families. We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our app, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately so we can remove it.
