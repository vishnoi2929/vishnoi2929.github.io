Key Features

AegisVault is designed from the ground up for maximum local protection and seamless user experience.

🛡️

Encrypted PIN Sandbox

Setup a local 6-digit PIN. Your files are isolated and copied into the App's private sandbox, completely invisible to the device gallery or default file managers.

🚫

Zero Internet Leakage

AegisVault works fully offline. We do not host remote servers. Your private data remains solely on your physical device, away from hackers and cloud compromises.

Play back your vault's private videos and audio tracks instantly with our premium built-in media player. Supports multiple screen orientations.
