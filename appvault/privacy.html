1. Introduction

Private Vault ("we", "our", "us") values and respects your privacy. Private Vault is a secure offline sandboxed file vault mobile application developed for Android devices. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning user information and local storage.

2. No Personal Data Collection

Private Vault does not collect, receive, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII). To use our App, you do not need to create an account, register, verify your email, link a phone number, or share any personal metadata. You remain fully anonymous.

3. Local Sandbox Storage

Your privacy is guaranteed by our architecture:

  • No Cloud Sync: All files you import (videos, audios, photos, and documents) are saved exclusively in your device's local memory within the App's private sandbox directory.
  • Passcode Encryption: Your App settings and 6-digit PIN are stored in secure local preferences on your device. We have no remote access to them.
  • Safe Disposal: Because your data is strictly local, uninstalling the App will delete all your imported vault files permanently. We cannot restore your data.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Private Vault displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and use anonymized device logs and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) for ad-targeting purposes, frequency capping, and usage analytics. This data processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policies. You can manage your ad personalization settings in your Android device's Settings menu.

5. Permissions Requested

We only ask for the permissions necessary to operate the security vault:

  • Photos & Videos / Storage Permission: Required to let you select and import media files from your public phone gallery into the secure sandbox.
  • Internet Access: Required strictly to load AdMob advertisements.
