Privacy Policy for Sabdwani

Last updated: July 12, 2026

At Sabdwani, accessible from the Google Play Store, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Sabdwani and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal identity information directly inside Sabdwani. However, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

2. Google AdMob (Advertising)

Our app uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob uses cookies and device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to customize and serve personalized ads. You can manage your device's ad settings to opt-out of personalized tracking.

For details on how Google uses your data, please read the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

3. Log Files

Sabdwani follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they use the app. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, and system information. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

4. Children's Privacy

Sabdwani does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our app, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.
